# GramFlow

Your Telegram channels as one timeline.

GramFlow is an Android app that shows the channels you follow on Telegram as a
single feed, newest first, instead of a list of chats. Posts, comments,
reactions, photos, videos and polls all read in one place, and you can still
message people and post to the channels you run.

GramFlow is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed
by Telegram.

## Features

- **One feed.** Every channel you follow, newest first, with your Telegram
  folders as tabs. Posts you have read stay out of the way, and read status
  stays in step with your other Telegram apps.
- **Channels.** A page for each channel with its posts, media, files and
  links, plus statistics for the channels you own or manage.
- **Messages.** Your direct messages and groups, with replies, forwards, pins,
  search and scheduled messages.
- **Posting.** Write to the channels you run: text, photos, videos, files,
  polls, stickers, voice messages and locations.
- **Activity.** Mentions, replies and reactions together, with notifications
  for what Telegram already notifies you about.
- **Guest mode.** Read public channels without signing in.
- **Links and sharing.** Telegram links open inside the app, and anything you
  share to GramFlow from another app opens in the composer.
- **Bookmarks, search and explore.**

## Install

GramFlow runs on Android 7.0 or newer. Download the latest APK from the
[Releases](https://github.com/mubarek-hassen-buli/GramFlow/releases) page and open it on
your phone. You may need to allow installs from your browser or file manager
the first time.

On iPhone (iOS 15 or newer), GramFlow isn't in the App Store. Each release
includes an `.ipa` file that you install with a free helper app such as
Sideloadly, SideStore or AltStore, using your own Apple Account. Apps installed
this way need a refresh once a week. The
[iPhone guide](docs/iphone.html) walks through it step by step.

Sign in with your Telegram phone number or by scanning a QR code from
Telegram on another device, or skip signing in and use guest mode.

## Privacy

GramFlow has no servers of its own. Signed in or as a guest, it talks only to
Telegram, and everything it stores stays on your phone. The privacy policy
and terms are in the app under Settings.
